Welcome

  • Move between questions with Previous and Next, or click a question in the list. You can change any answer until you end the exam.
  • Tick Mark for review to flag a question. Summary lists answered, unanswered and marked questions.
  • Click a drawing to open it in the image viewer. Zoom with the mouse wheel or the + and − buttons, and drag to pan.
  • Fill-in answers are numbers only, with a period as the decimal separator and no units. An answer within 1 % of the correct value scores full points.
  • Unless a question says otherwise, use MMGS units, 2 decimal places, and the material and density given in the question.
  • The exam ends when the timer reaches zero. Unanswered questions score zero.
