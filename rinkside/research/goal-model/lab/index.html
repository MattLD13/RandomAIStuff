The trees

The model is five copies (seeds) of 1,000 small trees. Every tree asks up to 14 yes/no questions about the shot and ends in a leaf that nudges the goal chance up or down in log-odds. The highlighted path is the route your shot takes.

SGP v2.1, frozen 2026-11-10: trained on the 2024-25 and 2025-26 regular seasons. Calibration as of –. Its clean test is games from 2026-11-11 on, scored every Monday. Method, tests and caveats: the research README.

Data: NHL play-by-play, shift charts and EDGE (prior-season shot speed). Coordinates in feet from center ice; the net is at x = 89.
